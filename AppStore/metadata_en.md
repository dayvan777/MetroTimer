## Name (up to 30 chars)

## Subtitle (up to 30 chars)

## Promotional text (up to 170 chars)
A Kyiv metro ride timer right in the Dynamic Island. Works with no internet — even in the tunnel. Warns you one station before you get off.

## Description

Reading, dozing off or listening to music on the metro? Metro Timer keeps track of the route for you.

Pick your departure station and your destination, tap “Start” the moment the train pulls out — and a countdown to your station appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with the number of stops left. One station before yours the phone vibrates: “Next stop: Khreshchatyk. Get ready to exit.”

QUICK START
Station search, pinned routes and recent trips get your daily ride going in two taps. Say “Last route in Metro Timer” to Siri and the app opens with the stations already filled in.

WORKS OFFLINE
There is no GPS underground — and none is needed. The app works out the train’s position from the official Kyiv Metro timetable (running times for every section, to the second). No servers, no ads, no analytics. Exactly one feature needs the internet — showing air-raid alerts — and it is off by default: you turn it on yourself.

DYNAMIC ISLAND AND LOCK SCREEN
The countdown lives in the Dynamic Island (iPhone 14 Pro and newer) and on the Lock Screen (any iPhone with iOS 16.1+). You can close the app — the timer and notifications keep working.

LINE CHANGES
Routes between lines are built automatically through the interchange stations. A separate notification tells you where to change. The wait for the connecting train is taken from the official service intervals, so an evening change is not counted as a rush-hour one — and when the train starts, one tap makes the countdown exact.

FIRST AND LAST TRAIN
If the metro is still closed, or the last train your way has already left, the app says so before you start — including the connecting train after a change.

SMART CORRECTION
On above-ground sections (the left-bank part of the red line, the Dnipro bridge on the green one) the app checks its estimate against GPS and corrects the drift itself. If the estimate slips underground, the −1/+1 buttons shift the route by one stop, straight from the Dynamic Island.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
Location is processed on device only, and only while the app is open. The app collects and transmits nothing: everything it stores stays on your phone.

FOR THOSE WHO WANT IT EXACT
Calibration mode lets you measure the real running times of your own line — the estimates then get more accurate for the routes you actually ride. The trip journal shows how closely the plan matched reality.

All 52 stations of the three Kyiv metro lines. Made in Ukraine.

Unofficial app; not affiliated with Kyiv Metro or the Kyiv City Administration. Data: Kyiv Open Data Portal; station coordinates © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
